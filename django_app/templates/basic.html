Teaching Artificial Intelligence in W3 Schools

In today's modern world, Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an important part of our daily lives. I believe that school should teach students about AI along with other subjects. Learning AI helps students understand new technologies, develop problem-solving skills, and improve their creativity. Students can learn how to use AI tools for education, research, and innovative projects. It also helps them prepare for future careers in technology. Teachers can explain the benefits and risks of AI and guide students to use it responsibly. By introducing AI at an early age, school can help students become more confident, knowledgeable, and ready for the future.
